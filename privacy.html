Privacy Policy

Why It's Like That publishing tools — last updated 16 September 2026.

What this covers

"Why It's Like That" is a video channel. Its owner uses a private, self-hosted publishing tool (the "app") to upload the channel's own videos to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. This policy describes what that app does with data.

Whose data the app handles

The app is used by one person: the owner of the channel. It connects only to the owner's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not collect data from viewers of the channel or from any third party.

What the app stores

To publish on the owner's behalf, the app stores the access tokens that YouTube, TikTok and Instagram issue when the owner authorises it, together with the account identifiers those platforms return. Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository and are used exclusively to upload videos and to keep the authorisation alive. The app stores the channel's own video files, titles and descriptions. It does not store passwords.

What the app does not do

The app does not read, collect or store data about other users of any platform. It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this site. It does not sell, share or transfer data to anyone.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept for as long as the owner keeps the app connected. The owner can revoke access at any time in the settings of the respective platform (YouTube: Google Account → Security → Third-party access; TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites). Revoking access invalidates the stored token immediately.
